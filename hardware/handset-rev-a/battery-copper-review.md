# Battery entry copper and fuse follow-up — 2026-09-27

The installed PCB now has a **1.2 mm main section** on the J1.1 → F2.1 battery-positive route. The connector escape, constrained passage beside C28 and final fuse escape remain 0.6 mm. No components moved, no vias were added, and all unrelated copper is preserved. This is a resistance improvement, not a qualified 5 A power path.

## Calculated improvement

`scripts/check_handset_battery_copper.py` extracts the actual BAT_PACK_POS segments and checks that they form a single serial B.Cu path between the actual J1.1 and F2.1 pad centers. Unsupported branches, disconnections or layer changes require re-auditing the model. The report covers only the copper before F2; it excludes the fuse, connector contacts, harness, VBAT distribution and ground return.

For **assumed 35 µm copper at 20°C**, using resistivity 1.724 × 10⁻⁸ Ω·m:

| Quantity | Previous route | Installed route |
|---|---:|---:|
| Centerline length | 20.6475 mm | 21.3546 mm |
| Calculated trace resistance | 16.951 mΩ | 12.893 mΩ |
| Drop at 3.302 A | 55.97 mV | 42.57 mV |
| Copper dissipation at 3.302 A | 184.8 mW | 140.6 mW |
| Copper dissipation at 5 A | 423.8 mW | 322.3 mW |

Resistance and calculated dissipation fall approximately **24%**. A direct shorter route crossed an existing +3V3 trace, so the final route retains the detour and gains width where clearance permits. The model also reports 18 µm copper and 70°C conductor-temperature sensitivity cases. These are assumptions, not an approved fabrication stackup or measured temperatures. It does not calculate temperature rise or establish a current rating; narrow sections remain important.

## Fuse decision remains open

The [Littelfuse 467 datasheet](https://www.littelfuse.com/assetdocs/fuse-467-datasheet?assetguid=4a59f034-1cca-460e-a5ba-e1e66247c76d) specifies 25% continuous-current derating and a further 0.8 factor at 70°C. Therefore:

| Fuse | Continuous screening at 25°C | At 70°C |
|---|---:|---:|
| Installed 1.5 A 046701.5NRHF | 1.125 A | 0.9 A |
| Candidate 5 A 0467005.NRHF | 3.75 A | 3.0 A |

The existing fuse cannot support the proposed load. The 5 A candidate fails the 3.302 A hot continuous screening and does not cover the existing 4.127 A margin target even at nominal temperature. The 70°C case refers to the fuse environment, not permission to operate or charge the battery at that temperature.

The same datasheet bounds opening at twice rated current to 5 seconds and at three times rated current to 0.2 seconds, at 25°C. The proposed pack's published 7–9 A PCM trip range is only 1.4–1.8 times a 5 A fuse rating. Those opening guarantees therefore do **not** establish coordination in that range. Nominal time-current curves or melting I²t alone cannot substitute for worst-case clearing and wiring-withstand evidence. F2 remains unchanged until the operating envelope, inrush, prospective fault current, PCM delay and harness/copper withstand are established.
